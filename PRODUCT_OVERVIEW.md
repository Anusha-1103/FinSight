# FinSight AI — Product Overview

FinSight AI is a commercial-grade, AI-powered personal wealth and finance management SaaS platform built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Express, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Redis, Cloudinary, and Gemini AI.

## Core Features
1. **Executive Financial Command Engine**:
   - 5-Factor Financial Health Index (0-100 composite score).
   - Net Worth Growth Trajectory Recharts area visualizer.
   - Day-of-week spending heatmap.
   - Budget burn rate tracking & goal target completion forecasting.
2. **Transaction Ledger & Receipt OCR Scanner**:
   - Full-text multi-filter transaction ledger with instant search.
   - Automated AI receipt image scanner powered by Gemini Vision OCR & Cloudinary storage.
3. **Conversational Gemini AI Advisor**:
   - Multi-turn financial advice drawer powered by Gemini API.
   - Automated spending anomaly detection & contextual AI callout alerts.
4. **Subscriptions & Recurring Overhead**:
   - Bill renewal alerts and monthly overhead cost calculator.
5. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**:
   - Separate `USER` and `ADMIN` roles with system performance metrics and security audit logs.
